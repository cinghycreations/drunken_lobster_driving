Drive like a Drunken Lobster and destroy everything!

Controls

Steer your car left and right using any of:
- Mouse ⬅️/➡️ or ⬆️/⬇️
- Keyboard arrow keys ⬅️/➡️ or ⬆️/⬇️
- Gamepad left stick ⬅️/➡️ or ⬆️/⬇️
- (Mobile) Swipe ⬅️/➡️ or ⬆️/⬇️

Modes

- Sequence: destroy streaks of identical buildings in a row. Increase multiplier with Skyscrapers and Nuclear plants
- Time Attack: destroy everything in the shortest possible time
